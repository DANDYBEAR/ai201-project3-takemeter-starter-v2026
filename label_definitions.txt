# The intent gate comes first: without a genuine information/advice request,
# use position even when the post contains checkable facts.

case_report: describes a discrete incident or external change, supports it with a checkable detail, and explicitly asks for explanation or action
worked_request: seeks advice for a concrete decision and supplies a material constraint, prior attempt, rejected option, or success criterion, but is not primarily investigating a discrete external incident
open_question: directly solicits information or experiences but provides no material constraint, prior attempt, success criterion, or checkable support for its premise
position: primarily argues, reacts, announces, promotes, or petitions; any question is rhetorical or secondary rather than a genuine request for information or advice
